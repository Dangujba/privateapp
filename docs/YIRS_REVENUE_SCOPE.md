# YIRS revenue scope represented in the system

The account and payment flows are arranged around categories published by Yobe State Internal Revenue Service and the Yobe State revenue framework.

## Payer groups represented

- employees whose personal income tax is remitted through employers under PAYE;
- self-employed persons such as professionals, contractors, traders and landlords under direct assessment;
- traders, manufacturers and service providers under the presumptive turnover regime where applicable;
- employers, private organisations and other organisations making statutory remittances;
- hotels, restaurants, shopping malls and event centres for applicable consumption charges;
- entertainment and media-related operators for applicable entertainment levies;
- landlords/property owners for applicable property-related revenue;
- transport and haulage operators;
- gaming, lottery and betting operators;
- livestock, agricultural, produce and other trade operators subject to state fees, levies or licences.

## Payment categories represented

PAYE remittance, direct/self-assessment, presumptive turnover tax, withholding tax remittance, capital gains tax, stamp duty, consumption tax, entertainment levy, property tax, road/transport revenue, gaming/lottery/betting revenue, trade/haulage fees, and licences/permits/statutory levies.

Reference basis: current Yobe State Internal Revenue Service tax-type and revenue-code publications, the Yobe State Revenue Codification Law 2021 and its amendment, and published schedules of state fees, levies and charges.
